Methodology

  • Pick several locations (busy junction, residential street, park, etc.).
  • Count vehicles passing in a fixed period and scale to vehicles per hour; note the share of heavy vehicles (trucks/buses).
  • Measure dust deposition by exposing a clean, weighed collection plate or jar for a day, then weigh again (mg/m²/day).
  • Repeat at similar times on several days and compare locations.
  • Run the anonymous survey to link measurements with how people feel.

Limitations

  • Small, non-random samples – results show patterns, not proof.
  • Dust deposition is not the same as PM2.5/PM10 concentration.
  • Weather, wind, rain and nearby construction affect dust.
  • Survey answers are self-reported and may be biased.
  • The hotspot score is a simple teaching index with arbitrary reference values, not an official air quality index.
  • The traffic–dust correlation pools all locations, so differences between places can drive it.
  • Data is stored only in this browser (localStorage).
