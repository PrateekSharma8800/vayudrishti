    Observations

    Observations are the readings you take in the field: count the vehicles passing a spot and measure the dust that settles there. VayuDrishti turns them into charts and ranks your spots from cleanest to most polluted. New here? to see a finished study first.

    How to take an observation
    1. Pick a spot such as a busy junction, a quiet residential street or a park gate, and use the same name every visit.
    2. Count vehicles. Stand somewhere safe and count every vehicle that passes for 10 minutes. Multiply by 6 to get vehicles per hour.
    3. Note the heavy ones. Of those vehicles, what share were trucks and buses? That is heavy vehicles (%).
    4. Measure dust. Leave a clean, weighed plate or jar in the open for 24 hours, then weigh the dust it collected (in milligrams) and divide by the plate's area in m². The result is dust deposition in mg/m²/day.
    5. Write it down here with the date and time of day. Repeat on several days and at several spots: more spots and more days make the ranking more trustworthy.

    Cannot measure today? Live conditions shows the air and traffic right now, and the sample data shows how a finished study looks.

    Methodology

    How to measure

    • Pick several locations (busy junction, residential street, park, etc.).
    • Count vehicles passing in a fixed period and scale to vehicles per hour; note the share of heavy vehicles (trucks and buses).
    • Measure dust deposition by exposing a clean, weighed collection plate or jar for a day, then weigh again (mg/m²/day).
    • Repeat at similar times on several days and compare locations.
    • Run the anonymous survey to link measurements with how people feel.

    Limitations

    • Small, non-random samples: results show patterns, not proof.
    • Dust deposition is not the same as PM2.5/PM10 concentration.
    • Weather, wind, rain and nearby construction affect dust.
    • Survey answers are self-reported and may be biased.
    • The hotspot score is a simple teaching index with arbitrary reference values, not an official air quality index.
    • The traffic–dust correlation pools all locations, so differences between places can drive it.
    • Data is stored only in this browser (localStorage).
